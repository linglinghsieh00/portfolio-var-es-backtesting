# Portfolio VaR & ES — Results Visualization

## Figure 1: Rolling 99% VaR vs Realized Loss
## Figure 1A — Full-Sample Rolling 99% VaR vs Realized Loss

This figure compares one-day-ahead 99% VaR forecasts from
Historical Simulation, Parametric Normal, and Monte Carlo Normal
against realized daily portfolio losses over the full backtesting period.

Red markers indicate Historical Simulation VaR exceptions.
## Figure 1B — 2020 COVID-19 Market Crisis

This figure compares realized daily portfolio losses with
one-day-ahead 99% VaR forecasts during the 2020 market crisis.

The objective is to examine how quickly each model adapts
to sudden changes in market risk.


## Figure 2: VaR Exception Rate Comparison

Comparison of observed one-day VaR exception rates against
the theoretical rates of 5% and 1%.

Coverage results should be interpreted alongside the
Kupiec and Christoffersen statistical tests.

## Figure 3: Annual Joint VaR–ES Score Differences
This figure compares annual average FZ0 score differences
between Gaussian risk models and Historical Simulation.

Positive differences indicate lower (better) average FZ0
scores for Historical Simulation.

The objective is to evaluate the time variation of
relative forecasting performance.

## Figure 4: Robustness Analysis
This figure evaluates how excluding 2018 and 2020
changes the average FZ0 score advantage of
Historical Simulation relative to Gaussian models.

The exclusion analysis is exploratory and does not
establish causal relationships.
