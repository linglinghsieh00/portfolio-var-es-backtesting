# Historical Simulation VaR & Expected Shortfall

## Objective
Estimate 1-day 95% and 99% Value at Risk (VaR) and Expected Shortfall (ES) for a five-ETF portfolio using Historical Simulation.

## Portfolio
- SPY: 40%
- QQQ: 25%
- IWM: 15%
- EFA: 10%
- EEM: 10%

## Methodology
- Historical daily returns
- 500-trading-day rolling estimation window
- Non-parametric Historical Simulation
- One-day-ahead out-of-sample forecasts

## Research Questions
1. How much could the portfolio lose over one trading day?
2. How severe are losses beyond the VaR threshold?
3. How do risk estimates change through time?
